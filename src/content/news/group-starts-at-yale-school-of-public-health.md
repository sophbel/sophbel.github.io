The next adventure begins. I am thrilled to be starting my group at the [**Yale School of Public Health**](https://www.linkedin.com/school/yalesph/) in the Department of Environmental Health Science. What a perfect home for someone like myself, with research sitting in the intersection of several disciplines!  

🧬 The Belman research group will use pathogen genomes to understand infectious disease dynamics, including how the environment impacts disease patterns and how existing microbial diversity both modifies and is modified by these effects. 

☀️ What do we mean by the environment? The environment includes longer term patterns and short term variability of things like like temperature, humidity, and precipitation. It also includes air pollutants both anthropogenic (vehicle emissions) and natural (dust storms), as well as the human populations and sociodemographic factors which can increase or mitigate these factors.  

🧠 🫁 What diseases? To start with, the diseases of focus will be bacterial respiratory diseases which include meningitis or pneumonia for example. Despite lower respiratory tract infections being some of the leading causes of death worldwide we have very little idea how the environment influences evolutionary selection in the microbial population, or can alter patterns of transmission, carriage, to local or invasive diseases.

If any of these topics sound interesting to you, you’re excited about exploring complex questions, using and developing computational models (statistical to machine learning), diving into genomes with bioinformatics, get in touch and we can explore potential projects together. I will be looking to grow a team grounded in curiousity, kindness, and scientific enthusiasm.
