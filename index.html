I am currently a Schmidt Science Fellow and a postdoctoral scientist in the Global Health Resilience group at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center.

My research explores infectious disease dynamics with a focus on how environmental and sociodemographic factors affect bacterial respiratory disease. By integrating earth systems data with infectious disease epidemiology and genomics, I am working to understand the drivers of disease spread.

Beyond scientific research, I am an avid runner, skier, rock climber, and cyclist.
